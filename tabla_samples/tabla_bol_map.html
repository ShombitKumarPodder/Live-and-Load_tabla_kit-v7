Bhamra index

Bars 2–6 of tabla bhamra index.mscz, written one matra (beat) per box. ऽ marks a silent matra. The score has no tempo mark, so MuseScore plays it at 120.

Teachers and gharanas differ on fingering, especially for Ta and Na, Ti and Ṭe, and Ge and Ghe. This map follows one common way of playing them. Where your teacher plays a stroke differently, go with your teacher.
